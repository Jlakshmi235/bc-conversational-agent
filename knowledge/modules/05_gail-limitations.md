# What the estimate does not tell the user

## Theory Tags
- PMT: frame uncertainty as a limitation of the tool, not a reason for helplessness; preserve self-efficacy by pointing to a next step.
- FTT: follow detail with one clear gist closing message.

## Tone Guidance
Neutral context.

## Included factors
The Gail/BCRAT model uses:
- Age
- Age at first menstrual period
- Age at first live birth
- Benign breast biopsy history
- Atypical hyperplasia
- Number of first-degree relatives with breast cancer
- Race or ethnicity

## Factors not fully represented
- second- and third-degree relatives, including paternal-side relatives such as aunts and grandmothers,
- relatives' ages at diagnosis,
- whether a relative had cancer in both breasts,
- breast density,
- known pathogenic genetic variants such as BRCA1 or BRCA2,
- every personal, hormonal, environmental, and imaging-related factor.

The model is not a diagnostic test. It may not be appropriate for:
- People with prior invasive breast cancer, DCIS, or LCIS.
- Certain hereditary cancer syndromes.
- Histories of chest radiation.

## Closing message
Different models are designed for different clinical situations. Choosing another model requires individualized clinical assessment; a healthcare professional can help determine whether another approach is appropriate.

## Closing Behavior
Offer a next topic, such as understanding the result or preparing for a clinician discussion.

## Safety
No diagnosis and no causal attribution.
