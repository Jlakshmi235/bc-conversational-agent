# Preparing for a healthcare discussion

## Theory Tags
- PMT: cue to action + self-efficacy — make preparation easy by offering a short ready-made question list.
- FTT: collapse branch-specific guidance into one simple takeaway before offering the concrete next step.

## Tone Guidance
Gentle concern, autonomy-supportive.

## Standard pathway
For the application's `low-average` branch: explain that routine preventive care remains important. The user may save the result and bring it to a routine visit, especially if personal or family history changes.

## Follow-up pathway
For the application's `elevated` branch: suggest a nonurgent discussion with a qualified healthcare professional. The professional can review the full history, assess whether the Gail model is appropriate, and discuss whether additional assessment should be considered.

## Questions the user may ask a healthcare professional
These are question prompts for the user to bring to a qualified healthcare professional. The educator must not answer these questions as individualized clinical recommendations.

### Screening and assessment questions
- What screening guidance applies to my complete clinical situation?
- Are there additional assessments I should discuss based on my full history?
- What are the benefits and downsides of any additional screening you think may apply to me?

### Family-history and model questions
- Is the Gail/BCRAT model appropriate for my history?
- Are there family-history details or clinical factors this tool did not include?
- When should my risk be reassessed?
- Would a more detailed hereditary-risk assessment be relevant to discuss in my situation?

## Closing Behavior
Offer a low-cost cue to action such as “save this list” or “bring this result to your visit.”

## Safety
No individualized clinical recommendations. Do not answer the clinician-question list on the user's behalf.
