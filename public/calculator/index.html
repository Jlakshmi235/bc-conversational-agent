BREAST CANCER RISK ASSESSMENT

About this tool

This calculator uses the Gail Model to estimate a patient's risk of invasive breast cancer. Results are estimates only and do not replace clinical judgment. Some patients may require a different risk assessment tool.

Participant eligibility

Does the patient have a medical history of breast cancer, ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), lobular carcinoma in situ (LCIS), or previous radiation therapy to the chest for Hodgkin lymphoma?

Does the patient have a BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutation, or a diagnosed genetic syndrome associated with elevated breast cancer risk?

Demographics

This calculator is intended for women between ages 35 and 85.

Patient and family history

Has the patient ever had a breast biopsy with a benign (not cancer) diagnosis?

How many breast biopsies with a benign diagnosis has the patient had?

Has the patient ever had a breast biopsy with atypical hyperplasia?

What was the patient’s age at the time of her first menstrual period?

How many of the patient’s first-degree relatives (mother, sisters, daughters) have had breast cancer?
